ThermoGrid AI

Concept · Private

Problem. AI data centers generate enormous, mostly-wasted heat, and workload scheduling is rarely designed with thermal output in mind.

Solution. A predictive thermal orchestration concept that schedules compute workloads with heat generation in mind — forecasting, storing, and routing useful heat rather than just dumping it as waste.

My role. Sole author of the concept and pitch. Presented as Team Nexora at Eureka 2026 (E-Cell / NewGen IEDC, CVRCE) — participated, not selected for finals.

  • Predictive modeling
  • Workload scheduling
  • Systems design

Code and pitch deck are private — not yet public.

Hotel Booking Demand

In progress · Local

Problem. Raw hotel booking data is messy and full of the kind of real-world noise that breaks naive analysis: missing values, inconsistent formats, outliers.

Solution. An end-to-end pipeline covering data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and feature engineering, built as preparation for machine learning modeling on booking demand.

My role. Built solo, end to end, as a hands-on way to practice the full data-science workflow before modeling.

  • Pandas
